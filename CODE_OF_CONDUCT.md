# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing opinions,
constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best
for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or
attention, trolling or insults, harassment, publishing others' private
information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing these standards. Report conduct
concerns privately through the security advisory contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly while
respecting the privacy and security of reporters.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for inappropriate behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.
